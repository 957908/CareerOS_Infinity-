## Approved Formula

$$\text{Priority Score} = 0.30 \cdot \text{SkillMatch} + 0.20 \cdot \text{ExperienceFit} + 0.15 \cdot \text{CareerFit} + 0.15 \cdot \text{ATSMatch} + 0.05 \cdot \text{SalaryFit} + 0.05 \cdot \text{LocationFit} + 0.05 \cdot \text{WorkModeFit} + 0.05 \cdot \text{Freshness}$$

Outputs explainable positive and negative match factors.
