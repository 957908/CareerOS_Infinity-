# CareerOS JobPilot — Part 4 Cover Letter Engine

**Module**: Cover Letter Generation  
**Date**: August 13, 2026  

---

## Specifications

- **Word Count**: 300–500 words.
- **Structure**:
  1. Opening & Position Statement
  2. Verified Candidate / Job Alignment
  3. Relevant Verified Experience & Projects
  4. Motivation & Alignment
  5. Professional Closing

- **Invariant**: `TRUTH > PERSONALIZATION > ATS`. Unverified skills (Kafka, Spark, AWS) are stripped.
