# CareerOS Infinity - Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Collection & Usage
CareerOS Infinity collects profile data to execute semantic job matches and recruitment recommendations.
*   **Collected Information:** Email addresses, full names, uploaded resume documents (raw text and structural JSON), and vectorized representations of career accomplishments.
*   **Security Bounds:** All personal identifiers are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

---

## 2. GDPR & CCPA Compliance
*   **Right to Erasure:** Users retain the absolute right to delete their profiles. Deletion triggers soft-delete cascades in relational tables and complete node purging inside the pgvector Knowledge Graph within 24 hours.
*   **Data Portability:** Users can download their parsed schemas profile in structured JSON formats directly from the dashboard settings panel.
