# CareerOS Infinity - Business Plan & Product Vision

## 1. Product Vision & Value Proposition

**CareerOS Infinity** is a high-performance career operations platform designed to automate every step of the career lifecycle. Rather than a set of disjointed tools (a resume editor, a separate tracking sheet, a mock interview practice site), CareerOS Infinity provides a unified workspace driven by autonomous agent teams.

### Core Value Pillars
*   **Privacy-First Approach:** Strictly isolated user profiles; no data sharing with third-party LLM training systems.
*   **Contextual Intelligence:** The platform retains the user's complete history, resumes, applications, and performance logs to deliver highly tailored recommendations.
*   **Frictionless Workflows:** Automates scheduling, tracking, drafting, and analysis, keeping the user in control of approvals.

---

## 2. Market Analysis & Target Segments

### 2.1 The Market Opportunity
With millions of active professionals navigating remote transitions, contract jobs, and changing technological landscapes, traditional job search processes are slow and inefficient.
*   **ATS Screening Hurdles:** Over 75% of resumes are filtered out by Automated Tracking Systems before human review.
*   **Skill Shift Latency:** Professionals struggle to identify the exact skills required for higher-paying positions.

### 2.2 Customer Segments
1.  **Sarah (Active Job Hunter):** High urgency, actively seeking senior roles. Demands fast resume parsing, ATS optimizations, and interview preparation.
2.  **David (Passive Career Planner):** Medium urgency. Focuses on long-term skill enhancement, profile optimization, and learning roadmaps.

## 3. Monetization Strategy

To balance growth and scalability, CareerOS Infinity leverages a hybrid **B2C SaaS & B2B Enterprise Licensing** pricing structure.

```
       +-----------------------------------------------------------+
       |                  CareerOS Infinity Model                  |
       +-----------------------------------------------------------+
                                     |
             +-----------------------+-----------------------+
             |                                               |
  +--------------------+                          +--------------------+
  |      B2C SaaS      |                          |   B2B Enterprise   |
  | (Freemium / Pro)   |                          |  (B2B Outplacement)|
  +--------------------+                          +--------------------+
```

### 3.1 B2C Tier Model
*   **Free Tier:**
    *   1 Base parsed resume.
    *   Up to 5 job match analyses per month.
    *   Basic application tracking board.
*   **Pro Tier ($29/month or $240/year):**
    *   Unlimited resume parsing and ATS optimizations.
    *   Active cover letter studio and recruiter email generator.
    *   Dynamic mock interviews (using WebSocket voice and text feedback).
    *   Daily job alerts and smart matching score reports.

### 3.2 B2B Enterprise Outplacement & Licensing
*   **University Partners:** Structured dashboard for career service centers to monitor student graduation success metrics.
*   **Corporate Outplacement:** Licensed packages provided by restructuring companies to departing personnel, facilitating rapid redeployments.
*   **Pricing:** Custom volume contract agreements starting at $15/user/month (minimum 100 seats).

---

## 4. Business Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Total B2C pro subscriptions + B2B active monthly license volume.
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to LTV Ratio:** Target >= 3.0 ratio within 12 months.
*   **User Retention (Churn Rate):** Target monthly B2C churn under 4.5%.
*   **Platform Engagement Score:** Ratio of job uploads to interview prep sessions started.
